What is AthenaUI?

AthenaUI is a next‑generation agentic AI platform designed to give users full control, transparency, and customization over their AI workflows. Built with modularity at its core, AthenaUI adapts to your needs — whether you're building automations, managing content, or developing advanced AI‑driven systems.

With AthenaUI, you’re not just using AI. You’re shaping it.

AthenaNavigator

AthenaNavigator is your command center — a visual, intuitive interface that lets you build, modify, and deploy AI workflows with ease. Drag‑and‑drop modules, connect logic, and watch your automations come to life.

Athena Code Studio

For developers and power users, Athena Code Studio provides a full scripting environment where you can write custom logic, extend AthenaUI’s capabilities, and integrate external APIs. Build your own amazing programs — Vibe Coding taken to the next level!

Group Chat — A Shared Space for Your Characters

AthenaUI includes a powerful Group Chat system that lets you speak with multiple characters at the same time. This isn’t just a chatroom — it’s a coordinated, multi‑agent environment where your characters interact with you and with each other in real time.

The character you speak with in private chat is the same persistent character you speak with in Group Chat, and the same character performing agent tasks behind the scenes. One character. One mind. One continuous identity.

Group Chat currently supports conversations only with the characters in your own AthenaUI environment. It does not support adding other users or their characters.

Security & Transparency

AthenaUI is built on a simple principle: you should always know what your AI is doing, why it’s doing it, and what it touched along the way. No hidden processes. No silent data collection. No opaque decision-making. Every action taken by an agent is visible, traceable, and fully under your control.

AI Governance Built Into the Core

AthenaUI is designed with governance at its foundation. As multi-agent systems grow more capable, the need for transparent, auditable, and user-controlled AI becomes essential — not just in v2.6, but in every version that follows.

Customization

Every part of AthenaUI is customizable — from agent behavior, to workflow logic, to interface layout, to memory preferences, to automation triggers. AthenaUI adapts to your style, your needs, and your goals.
